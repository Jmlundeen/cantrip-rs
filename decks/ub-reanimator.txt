# UB Reanimator: put something enormous into the graveyard on turn one, and put it onto the battlefield on turn two.
#
# Forty cards, singleton but for the basics, so a game of it draws a different part of the plan every time. Entomb and
# the Troll's swampcycling bury a threat on purpose; the Frog, Jace and Frantic Search do it while digging; Collective
# Brutality makes a discard out of a spell worth casting anyway. Six ways to bring one back, of which Goryo's Vengeance
# and Shallow Grave do it at instant speed and only until end of turn.
#
#   tools\bots-deck-duel.cmd -Deck1 ub-reanimator -Deck2 rw-aggro -Games 400
